BetterGovPH design language#

The visual language of the generated page — typography roles (Figtree for display, Inter for text, JetBrains Mono for metadata), BetterGov blue, white paper, rule-based tables, masthead/subnav/footer structure — is adapted from BetterGovPH's transparency-portal repository, which is MIT-licensed (© 2026 BetterGov.ph) and mirrors budget.bettergov.ph. That MIT licence permits reuse and adaptation; the adaptation in this repository is itself released under the MIT licence in LICENSE.

Brand assets (BetterGovPH)#

The BetterGov logo, emblem and favicon are the brand property of BetterGovPH and are not covered by this project's MIT licence. They are used here, unmodified and with attribution, to identify this work as part of the BetterGovPH open-data design family. Nothing in this repository implies that this project is an official BetterGov.ph product or that BetterGovPH endorses it. Before any independent deployment, the deploying party should obtain the brand owner's approval for the mark's use, or replace it with their own mark. If the brand owner asks for a change, the assets will be updated or removed.

Fonts#

Three woff2 files under draft/assets/fonts/ are the latin variable subsets of Figtree, Inter and JetBrains Mono, taken from Google Fonts and inlined into the page as data URIs at build time (they are unmodified font software). Each is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (OFL 1.1), which permits embedding and redistribution in this form; the fonts are not sold separately, no Reserved Font Name is used, and the required copyright notices are the ones listed in the table above. The OFL text for each family is available from the Google Fonts repository (google/fonts, ofl/<family>/OFL.txt) and from the font projects themselves.

Icons#

Interface icons come from the open-source Lucide icon set. The Lucide project's licence is the ISC License (© Lucide Icons and Contributors); Lucide also states that a listed subset of its icons derives from the Feather project and remains under the MIT License (© 2013-present Cole Bemis). Both are permissive and compatible with this repository's MIT licence. The icon sprite is vendored (not loaded from a CDN), so the page makes no external requests; the pinned version and the vendored notice live in draft/assets/brand/.

Philippine region geometry#

draft/assets/geo.json is a simplified, projected Philippine region boundary file (18 PSGC 3Q 2026 regions, including NIR and BARMM) built by tools/build-geo.py from faeldon/philippines-json-maps release v1.1.1 (TopoJSON, level regions, resolution hires).

  • That repository's scripts and map files are MIT-licensed (© James Faeldon).
  • The boundaries are licensed CC BY 3.0 IGO: they are derived from NAMRIA and the Philippine Statistics Authority data, via OCHA's Philippines Common Operational Dataset for Administrative Boundaries (COD-AB). Required attribution: "Province boundaries: © NAMRIA and the Philippine Statistics Authority, via OCHA's Philippines COD-AB, licensed CC BY 3.0 IGO."
  • This repository inlines a simplified derivative of that data for a schema-level choropleth (18 PSGC regions) and for the provider service-area overlay (1,642 city / municipality polygons in draft/assets/coverage-geo.json, built from the same release's province/district TopoJSON files); it is not an authoritative boundary dataset. Full provenance — source release, archive and per-file SHA-256 hashes, the pinned checksums file, projection, simplification parameters and evaluated alternatives — is recorded in draft/assets/geo-sources.json. Reusers of the geometry should preserve the CC BY 3.0 IGO attribution above.

Department of Energy (DOE) workbook data#

The rates, utility list and aggregates in data/seed/ are derived from the DOE "Electricity Rates & Power Market Monitor" workbook — a public Google Sheet embedded on doe.gov.ph/data-and-prices/electricity — retrieved through its public CSV export on 2026-11-10 (dataset version 2026-11-10).

  • No dataset-specific licence has been confirmed with DOE. The DOE website footer states that its content is in the public domain "unless otherwise stated", but this project does not treat that general statement as a confirmed licence for the workbook, and this repository does not assert a licence over the workbook or over the figures extracted from it.
  • What this repository publishes is a derived factual extract (utility registry, monthly rate observations, region aggregates) together with provenance: the DOE page and sheet URLs, the retrieval timestamp, the row and tab each value came from, and a SHA-256 hash of the raw tab bytes. The raw byte snapshots in data/raw/doe-rates/ exist so that a published number can be reproduced from exactly the publisher bytes we saw; they are not a republication of the workbook as a dataset.
  • No endorsement by DOE is implied. DOE is the publisher of the source workbook; it has not reviewed, approved or licensed this extract.
  • Reusers should cite the DOE workbook and this retrieval date, and should satisfy themselves about DOE's terms. Confirming the workbook's ownership, refresh cadence and licence/citation terms with DOE is a week-1 partnership ask recorded in research/data-landscape.md.
  • If DOE asks for changes to how the source data is presented or redistributed, the project will comply and update this notice.

Generated files — data/seed/*.csv, data/seed/page-data.json, data/seed/validation.json (built by tools/ingest-doe-rates.py), the service-area dataset (tools/build-service-areas.py), the static JSON API under draft/api/v1/ (built by tools/build-api.py), the docs site under draft/docs/ (built by tools/build-docs.py) and the page draft/map-table-prototype.html (built by tools/build-map-page.py) — are produced by this project's build tooling. The MIT licence applies to the generator code and to the project's own markup/design; it does not relicense the underlying DOE figures, whose terms remain as described above.

Service-area coverage data (data/seed/service_areas.csv)#

The provider filter draws each distribution utility's service area. That layer is derived data, not part of the DOE rate workbook, and it carries its own licences:

  • Meralco — Republic Act No. 12146 (approved 11 April 2025), Section 1, which renews the Meralco distribution franchise and enumerates its territory. The text was retrieved from the Supreme Court E-Library (https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocsfriendly/2/98850, 2026-11-10). Philippine laws are public documents; the official text is not subject to copyright. The franchise area is encoded LGU-by-LGU against PSGC 3Q 2026; the law wins over the secondary sources below.
  • Other distribution utilities — LGU-level franchise areas from the OpenStreetMap Wiki page "Power networks/Philippines/Operators", revision 2836797 (2025-04-14), which tabulates franchise areas "per Department of Energy". That page is licensed CC BY-SA 2.0; required attribution: "Service areas: © OpenStreetMap Wiki contributors, 'Power networks/Philippines/Operators', revision 2836797 (2025-04-14), CC BY-SA 2.0."
  • Supplementary LGU areas for 22 utilities the OSM page lists without a franchise-area table come from Wikipedia, "List of electric distribution utilities in the Philippines", revision 1374171231 (2026-09-10), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0; required attribution: "Additional service areas: © Wikipedia contributors, 'List of electric distribution utilities in the Philippines', revision 1374171231 (2026-09-10), CC BY-SA 4.0."
  • Fallback — where no LGU-level area is sourced, the DOE workbook's region assignment is published as a labelled region-level area. The 2026 edition of the dataset needed zero fallbacks; the mechanism and its label remain in the contract.
  • The combined coverage dataset (data/seed/service_areas.csv, data/seed/service_area_sources.csv and the derived LGU overlay) is a CC BY-SA derivative of the sources above: attribution to the OpenStreetMap Wiki and Wikipedia contributors (and share-alike for the derived coverage database) is required. The project's MIT licence applies to the assembler code and to the page's own markup, not to this derived dataset. Per-row source ids and revision/retrieval metadata are published in service_area_sources.csv; the extraction method, pinned wikitext snapshots and known gaps are documented in research/coverage/README.md.
  • The coverage data is not an official DOE publication and implies no endorsement. It records partial-coverage notes where an LGU is split between utilities, and documents PSGC 3Q 2026 region reassignments instead of forcing legacy DOE region labels.
