LGU-level service areas for the 141 distribution utilities

research/coverage/README.md · 555 lines · 29 KB · 5,093 words

Download raw All docs

Every distribution utility (DU) in data/seed/utilities.csv has a DOE-assigned region, but real franchise areas are far finer: co-ops cover part of a province, one province can hold several co-ops, and a few DUs span regions. This folder produces an auditable, reproducible LGU (municipality / city) coverage table for all 141 DUs so the page's provider filter can draw each provider's operating area, not just Meralco's.

At a glance#
Utilities covered 141 / 141 (100%) have at least one LGU row; 0 need a region fallback
Rows 1,630 LGU rows (1,433 from the OSM page, 197 from the Wikipedia supplement)
LGUs mapped 1,585 distinct PSGC LGUs out of 1,642 (96.5%); 57 remain uncovered, listed below
Primary source OpenStreetMap Wiki Power networks/Philippines/Operators, revision 2836797 (2025-04-14), CC BY-SA 2.0
Supplementary source Wikipedia List of electric distribution utilities in the Philippines, revision 1374171231 (2026-09-10), CC BY-SA 4.0 — used only for the 22 DUs the OSM page lists without a franchise table
Codes All emitted PSGC codes are canonical zero-padded 10-digit strings (e.g. Angat 0301401000, Bulacan 0301400000)
Deterministic python3 research/coverage/extract.py; pinned revisions + committed wikitext snapshots

Status: ready for the Lead's merge. MERALCO rows are OSM-derived and flagged law-override-pending (RA 12146); the Lead's merge replaces them with the law-level list (a colleague encodes RA 12146 Section 1 in research/service-areas.json: 108 LGUs + 17 Pampanga barangays).

Merge contract with tools/build-service-areas.py#

The raw file is the merge tool's first input. Checked against the tool and the pinned LGU geometry (draft/assets/coverage-geo.json): 1,540 / 1,540 visible rows resolve to a geometry LGU (0 misses) for all utilities except MERALCO (the tool deliberately ignores our MERALCO rows and substitutes RA 12146). Two integration notes for the merge:

  1. Cross-utility LGU overlaps are intentional. 42 LGUs are claimed by more than one utility here because the sources describe partial coverage (e.g. San Carlos City is CENPELCO's while barangays Cruz and Bolingit are DECORP's; Calanasan is KAELCO's while two barangays are CAGELCO II's; 18 FIBECO/MAGELCO/ SUKELCO/PPALMA/CLPC/COLIGHT partial overlaps in Mindanao; CEDC's Clark Freeport portions inside PELCO II/TARELCO II/AEC areas; MORE-POWER's 2022 expansion into ILECO areas). The merge should therefore de-duplicate on (utility_code, adm3_psgc), not on adm3_psgc alone — the current tools/build-service-areas.py uses a global seen set and would report these 42 legitimate overlaps as duplicates.
  2. The published file adds area_level=barangay rows (MERALCO Pampanga, from RA 12146) and may add area_level=region fallbacks; this raw extraction has 0 of either.

Output schema#

data/seed/service_areas.csv — header matches the column contract of the Lead's merge tool (tools/build-service-areas.py AREA_HEADER) and tools/validate.py:

text
utility_code,source_id,area_level,region_psgc,province_psgc,province_name,adm3_psgc,lgu_name,adm4_psgc,barangay_name,coverage_note

adm4_psgc and barangay_name are always empty in this raw extraction: it is LGU-level only. They exist for the published dataset, where the Lead's merge adds MERALCO's 17 RA 12146 Pampanga barangays as area_level=barangay rows.

  • utility_code — one of the 141 DOE workbook codes.
  • source_id — SRC-OSM-OPERATORS or SRC-WIKI-DUS for LGU rows; SRC-DOE-RATES for an area_level=region fallback (none are needed in the current build).
  • area_level — lgu (one row per covered LGU) or region (fallback only).
  • region_psgc — the service area's PSGC region (not the DU's DOE region), so joins to LGU geometry work; cross-region rows therefore differ from the DU's DOE region and say so in coverage_note.
  • province_psgc / province_name — the LGU's adm2 from the PSGC registry. For NCR and the independent City of Isabela the adm2 is a district / city ("NCR, Second District", "City of Isabela"); the "(Not a Province)" marker is dropped from the name only.
  • adm4_psgc / barangay_name — empty here (LGU-level extraction only); the Lead's merge fills them for the RA 12146 barangay rows.
  • coverage_note — "" normally; otherwise cross-region, partial barangay coverage (listed in source), a specific partial-coverage explanation, and/or law-override-pending (RA 12146).
  • Sorted by utility_code, then LGU PSGC; no duplicate (utility_code, adm3_psgc).

data/seed/service_area_sources.csv (header exactly as specified) has four rows: SRC-OSM-OPERATORS, SRC-WIKI-DUS, SRC-DOE-RATES (region fallback) and SRC-RA-12146 (placeholder for the Lead's Meralco override).

Sources, revisions and licences#

SRC-OSM-OPERATORS (primary)#

  • OpenStreetMap Wiki, Power networks/Philippines/Operators, https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Power_networks/Philippines/Operators
  • Revision 2836797, edited 2025-04-14 by Kjon; retrieved 2026-11-10.
  • Licence: CC BY-SA 2.0. The page tabulates operator franchise areas as "per Department of Energy", with a municipality-level table for most operators.
  • Snapshots: snapshots/osm-wiki-2836797.wikitext (+ provenance json).

Structure used: 15 region sections with wikitable rows (Name (abbreviation) | Franchise areas | Notes), plus bullet-only sections: Nationwide (NGCP), Metro Manila (Meralco), MIMAROPA and Bicol. Operators repeat across sections (Meralco 3×, CAGELCO II, AURELCO, BASELCO, FIBECO, LASURECO, SUKELCO, ZAMSURECO-I, COTELCO-PPALMA, QUEZELCO-I bullet) — rows are merged per operator and areas unioned.

SRC-WIKI-DUS (supplementary, 22 utilities only)#

  • Wikipedia, List of electric distribution utilities in the Philippines, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_electric_distribution_utilities_in_the_Philippines
  • Revision 1374171231, edited 2026-09-10 by FiveGeekabytes; retrieved 2026-11-10.
  • Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Used only where the OSM page has no usable LGU list for a DU; the OSM page leaves the Bicol and MIMAROPA sections as bullet lists without franchise areas (and has no row for BANELCO / PROSIELCO / CEDC). 22 utilities are supplemented: ALECO, BANELCO, BISELCO, CANORECO, CASURECO1–4, CEDC, FICELCO, LUBELCO, MARELCO, MASELCO, OMECO, ORMECO, PALECO, PROSIELCO, ROMELCO, SORECO1, SORECO2, TIELCO, TISELCO.
  • Every supplemented row is marked source_id=SRC-WIKI-DUS so it can be attributed and re-checked. It is also cross-checked against the OSM result for the 116 utilities present in both sources (see "Cross-check" below).

SRC-DOE-RATES (region fallback only)#

The DOE workbook behind data/seed/utilities.csv gives each DU one region; it is the fallback source for area_level=region rows. None are needed: all 141 DUs have LGU-level coverage in this build. Licence: no explicit DOE dataset licence; see data/seed/sources.csv ("public records; reuse terms to be confirmed").

SRC-RA-12146 (placeholder for the Lead's merge)#

RA 12146 (Meralco franchise renewal), official text via the Supreme Court e-Library; the colleague's law encoding is research/service-areas.json (not edited here). MERALCO rows below are OSM-derived and flagged law-override-pending (RA 12146); the merge should replace them.

Method#

  1. PSGC LGU registry. Built from the pinned PSGC 3Q 2026 TopoJSON archive (philippines-json-maps v1.1.1, the file cached by tools/build-geo.py): topojson/provdists/hires/municities-provdist-<adm2>.topo.0.1.json gives 1,642 LGUs (149 cities + 1,493 municipalities) with adm1_psgc, adm2_psgc, adm3_psgc, adm3_en; province names come from topojson/regions/hires/*.json. Codes are zero-padded to 10 digits.
  2. Extract the OSM table and bullets from the pinned wikitext (osm-operators.json.raw_rows keeps every row/section verbatim). Bullet sections contribute operators and franchise hints, e.g. Quezon I Electric Cooperative (QUEZELCO-I) (Del Gallego, Camarines Sur).
  3. Merge operators across region sections by display name (the abbreviation in parentheses is kept separately) and union their areas.
  4. Parse franchise-area cells into LGU items:
    • A, B and C in <Province> → each item resolved inside that province;
    • A, B and C in <LGU>, <Province> → barangay-level coverage: one partial row for the parent LGU with coverage_note = "partial barangay coverage (listed in source)" (e.g. DECORP: Cruz and Bolingit in San Carlos City; QUIRELCO: 18 barangays in San Agustin; Meralco: Candaba / Apalit);
    • parts of <LGU> / portions of <LGU> → partial LGU row;
    • a bare province name (alone in its cell) → whole-province expansion (this is how "Benguet", "Bulacan", "Cavite", "Zamboanga del Sur", etc. work);
    • province including LGU → province expansion plus the extra LGU (e.g. "Capiz including Roxas City");
    • notes' whole-LGU exclusions are applied, e.g. LANECO excludes Iligan City, Pantao Ragat, Pantar (served by ILPI/LASURECO); LUELCO excludes Bauang, San Fernando, San Juan; ANTECO excludes Libertad, Pandan; LASURECO excludes Wao; DASURECO excludes Davao City; ZANECO/ZAMSURECO-I exclude the LGUs noted as served by ZAMSURECO-II; MAGELCO excludes Cotabato City; SUKELCO excludes Lutayan.
  5. Resolve LGU names to PSGC. Order of evidence: exact name / known alias → province context stated in the source text → the operator's DOE region (only when that narrows the field to one candidate). Ambiguous names are never guessed: the build reports 0 unresolved LGU names and 3 unmapped source items (below). Name handling covers City of X / X City forms, Sto. = Santo / Sta. = Santa expansion, accent folding (Peñablanca), hyphen folding (Impasug-ong, Miag-ao, M'Lang), and the PSGC 3Q 2026 names that differ from the source text (Sawata for San Isidro, Davao del Norte; Pagagawan for Datu Montawal; Gen. S.K. Pendatun; Salvador Benedicto; Pigkawayan; Cabanglasan; Enrique B. Magalona; Dr. Jose P. Rizal for Rizal, Palawan).
  6. Cross-region check: every LGU's adm1_psgc is compared with the DU's DOE region. Mismatches are kept and flagged cross-region (not dropped) because several are genuine franchises: Meralco (NCR DU serving Regions III/IV-A), AURELCO (Region III DU serving Dinapigue, Isabela), QUEZELCO-I (Region IV-A DU serving Del Gallego, Camarines Sur), CAGELCO-II (Region II DU serving Apayao), FIBECO (Region X DU serving Wao, Lanao del Sur), LASURECO (BARMM DU serving Pantao Ragat/Pantar, Lanao del Norte), PPALMA (Region XII DU serving Maguindanao LGUs), SUKELCO (Region XII DU serving Maguindanao del Sur), ZAMSURECO-I (Region IX DU serving Don Victoriano, Misamis Occidental), BASELCO (BARMM DU serving Isabela City, Region IX), SULECO/SIASELCO (BARMM DUs serving Sulu, which PSGC 3Q 2026 moved to Region IX) and PROSIELCO (DOE Region VII DU serving Siquijor, which PSGC 3Q 2026 moved to NIR).
  7. Meralco: OSM rows are kept but every row carries law-override-pending (RA 12146); the Lead's merge replaces them with the RA 12146 list.
  8. Validate: every one of the 141 codes is present; no duplicate (utility_code, adm3_psgc); every adm3_psgc exists in the registry; every region code is one of the 18 PSGC regions; ≥90% of utilities have LGU rows. The script exits non-zero if any check fails.

Ambiguities and unmapped source items#

  • Unresolved LGU names: none (0 in the current build).
  • Unmapped franchise-area items (kept in the report, no LGU invented):
    • Clark Freeport Zone (CEDC) — a special economic zone, not an LGU; CEDC's LGU rows come from the Wikipedia supplement (Clark portions in Angeles, Mabalacat, Porac, Bamban, Capas).
    • First Philippine Industrial Park (Meralco) — an industrial estate inside Sto. Tomas, Batangas, which is already covered by the Meralco row set.
    • Tungo (LEYECO-3) — a barangay listed alongside LGUs in the source; not an LGU, so it is not mapped.
  • Source operators that are not DUs in the 141-row workbook (extracted for audit, not used): NGCP (transmission), APEC (concessionaire inside the ALECO franchise; ALECO/APEC are one franchise on the sources, and the DOE workbook has only ALECO), SPUG (NAPOCOR missionary electrification), Subic Enerzone and LIMA Enerzone (freeports/industrial parks), and SIQUELCO (the OSM page's name for Siquijor's DU; the workbook code is PROSIELCO, which is used instead).

Known gaps#

57 of the 1,642 LGUs are not claimed by any of the 141 DUs. Most are explained:

  • NCR 17 LGUs — the OSM page's Metro Manila section is a bare bullet with no LGU list, so no NCR LGU appears here. The Lead's MERALCO RA 12146 override adds all 17; that is why Meralco is marked "law-override-pending" rather than skipped.
  • 11 Sulu LGUs — SULECO's row lists only 7 of Sulu's 18 LGUs and SIASELCO covers Siasi; the remaining LGUs are unlisted in the source (the Sulu franchise table is incomplete).
  • 8 Special Geographic Area LGUs (BARMM, in Cotabato) — new 2024 municipalities not covered by any DU row in the sources.
  • Romblon 3 (Banton, Concepcion, Corcuera) and Turtle Islands, Tawi-Tawi — small island/local systems that are not DUs in the 141-row DOE workbook.
  • Kalayaan, Palawan — the Spratly municipality has no distribution utility row.
  • OSM-page omissions in otherwise-covered provinces: Samar 3 (Basey, Paranas, Zumarraga — Wikipedia attributes them to SAMELCO-II), Basilan 2 (Maluso, Sumisip — Wikipedia attributes them to BASELCO), plus single LGUs in Zambales (San Marcelino), Batangas (Balete, Mataasnakahoy), Laguna (Alaminos, Rizal), Quezon (Dolores), Cebu (San Remigio), Leyte (Tunga), Misamis Oriental (Talisayan), Cagayan (Calayan) and Pangasinan (Sison). These are left as gaps rather than mixed in from the secondary source, because for those DUs the primary table exists and is authoritative for the franchise list.
  • PALECO list is 19 LGUs: Palawan's 24 minus the 4 Calamianes LGUs served by BISELCO and minus Kalayaan (no DU) — consistent with the source.
  • ALECO is 18 LGUs (all Albay): the Wikipedia source lists the combined ALECO/APEC franchise; APEC is not a separate DOE workbook row (see coverage_note on those rows).

Cross-check against the Wikipedia list#

For the 116 utilities present in both sources the LGU sets were compared: 72 identical, 44 differing (details in osm-operators.json.report. wiki_cross_check). The differences are mostly errors/incompleteness in the secondary source and were not used to overwrite the primary data, e.g.:

  • Wikipedia's DLPC row includes LGUs from Davao del Norte, Cotabato and Davao del Sur (the OSM table gives DLPC Davao City + 4 Davao del Norte LGUs);
  • its MORESCO-I row includes Baungon/Cagayan de Oro/Iligan, which belong to other DUs; its LUELCO row lists the whole of La Union (24 entries) while the cooperative's area is La Union minus Bauang/San Fernando/San Juan;
  • it adds a few real OSM omissions (SAMELCO-II's Basey/Paranas/Zumarraga, ZAMECO-II's San Marcelino) that are listed under "Known gaps" rather than mixed in;
  • it disagrees on CEPALCO (Jasaan/Villanueva) and CENECO/NEPC, where the sources themselves conflict.

The comparison table is kept in the JSON report; SRC-WIKI-DUS rows in the CSV are only the 22 utilities where the OSM page has no LGU data at all.

Reproduction#

text
# from the repository root; needs the pinned PSGC TopoJSON archive, which
# tools/build-geo.py already caches (or pass --topojson PATH)
python3 research/coverage/extract.py

The script uses the committed wikitext snapshots by default (no network) and verifies their pinned revision ids. --refresh refetches the two pinned revisions; --topojson PATH overrides the archive location. Outputs are written in one pass; data/seed/service_areas.csv is asserted byte-identical to research/coverage/osm-service-areas.csv. The CSVs are deterministic; the JSON report carries generated_at/retrieved_at timestamps.

Licence and attribution#

Rows carry source_id so the page/API can attribute each area. The combined service-area dataset is a derivative of CC BY-SA sources: redistributing it requires attribution and share-alike terms compatible with the source licences.
